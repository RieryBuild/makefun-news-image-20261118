## The six timeline stories (photo nodes, in the order they broke)

1. **OVERNIGHT — CRIMEA BRIDGE FALLS.** Ukraine's strike drops the road
   arch of the 19 km Kerch bridge into the sea, the second strike in two
   days; Russia answers by pounding Kyiv's bridges and other
   infrastructure, and says it intercepted a record 1,441 drones.
   Sources: Reuters, 17 Nov ("Ukraine strikes Crimean bridge for second
   day as Russia pounds Kyiv bridges"); Atlantic Council UkraineAlert,
   17 Nov; Bloomberg via Military.com, 17 Nov.
2. **FIRST LIGHT — GULF ON EDGE.** Iran's Revolutionary Guard launches
   drones at the US-operated radar at Sheikh Isa air base, Bahrain; the
   drones are intercepted, no damage. US fighter jets strike an IRGC
   missile launch site on Qeshm Island in reply. Kuwait also reports
   drone attacks.
   Sources: Al Jazeera liveblog, 18 Nov 00:24 UTC; Particle News,
   18 Nov 00:01 UTC; US Central Command statement.
3. **MORNING — MONGOLIA'S PRESIDENT QUITS.** President Khurelsukh
   Ukhnaa announces his resignation after the opposition accused him of
   taking $25m from a railway contract; he denies wrongdoing. The
   resignation needs parliament's approval; the speaker would act as
   president and an election must follow within four months.
   Sources: Reuters via BusinessWorld, 17 Nov; Associated Press, 17 Nov.
4. **MIDDAY — STUDENTS TAKE SERBIA'S PARLIAMENT.** The Student List's
   deputies enter parliament after winning 140 of 250 seats in the
   25 October election, ending 14 years of rule by the Serbian
   Progressive Party.
   Sources: Reuters, 17 Nov 12:43 UTC.
5. **AFTERNOON — TRUMP IN MANILA, APEC OPENS.** Trump meets the Quad
   leaders (India, Japan, Australia) for the first time in two years,
   then meets President Marcos in Manila before flying to Shenzhen,
   where the APEC leaders' meeting opens 18–19 Nov ahead of Thursday's
   Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral.
   Sources: Reuters via BusinessWorld, 17 Nov; AJP, 18 Nov 08:53.
6. **NIGHT — NVIDIA SAYS NO TO HUANG.** Nvidia reports a record
   quarterly revenue of $115.8bn, up 103% year on year; on the earnings
   call the board says it has no intention of reinstating founder Jensen
   Huang, who had said he would be "honoured" to return. Interim CEO
   Colette Kress stays in charge.
   Sources: CNN, 17 Nov; CoinTelegraph, 18 Nov 00:16 UTC; Ctech,
   18 Nov 00:30 UTC.
